# Contributing to CveToad

Copyright © 2025-2026 Keerthana Purushotham.

Thank you for your interest in contributing to CveToad.

CveToad uses a dual-licensing model:

1. GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (`AGPL-3.0-only`); or
2. A separately negotiated commercial license.

To preserve the project's ability to distribute accepted contributions
under both alternatives, substantive contributions require affirmative
acceptance of the contributor agreement described below.

## 1. Contributions Covered by This Policy

A "Contribution" is copyrightable or patentable material intentionally
submitted for inclusion in the project, including:

- source code;
- tests and build scripts;
- documentation;
- diagrams and technical specifications;
- patches;
- substantive design implementations; and
- modifications to existing project materials.

General feedback, bug reports, feature requests, questions, and ideas
submitted through issues or discussions are not treated as copyright
assignments.

## 2. Contributor Agreement Required

Before a substantive Contribution can be accepted or merged, the
contributor must affirmatively enter into the project's separate
Contributor Copyright Assignment Agreement or other contributor agreement
approved by the project owner.

Submitting an issue, comment, discussion post, or pull request does not by
itself transfer copyright, patent rights, or other intellectual-property
ownership.

A Contribution for which the required contributor agreement has not been
completed may be reviewed, discussed, or declined, but it will not be
merged into the project.

## 3. Rights Required for Dual Licensing

The contributor agreement must give the project owner sufficient rights to:

- reproduce, modify, and distribute the Contribution;
- include the Contribution in the AGPLv3 version of the project;
- include the Contribution in separately licensed commercial versions;
- sublicense or relicense the Contribution;
- enforce rights associated with the Contribution; and
- maintain the project's dual-licensing model.

If the project requires ownership of an accepted Contribution, the
copyright assignment must be made through a separately accepted Copyright
Assignment Agreement.

Where permitted by law, that agreement may also include:

- a waiver or agreement not to assert applicable moral rights;
- an irrevocable patent license for patent claims necessarily infringed by
  the Contribution; and
- a non-exclusive license back allowing the contributor to continue using
  their original Contribution.

No assignment should be inferred merely from participation in the
repository.

## 4. Contributor Representations

By submitting a Contribution, you represent that:

1. You created the Contribution or possess all rights necessary to submit it.
2. You have authority to enter into the applicable contributor agreement.
3. The Contribution does not knowingly infringe third-party copyright,
   patent, trademark, trade-secret, privacy, or other rights.
4. You have complied with applicable employment, consulting, university,
   funding, and intellectual-property obligations.
5. You have obtained any employer or institutional authorization required
   to make the Contribution.
6. You have disclosed all third-party code, data, licenses, and notices
   included in the Contribution.
7. The Contribution does not contain confidential, unlawfully obtained,
   export-controlled, or improperly disclosed material.

Do not submit employer-owned code, confidential information, credentials,
personal data, or material you are not authorized to contribute.

## 5. Third-Party Materials

Contributions containing third-party material must clearly identify:

- the material's source;
- its copyright holder;
- its applicable license;
- all required attribution or notice text; and
- any restrictions relevant to use or redistribution.

Third-party material may be rejected if its terms are incompatible with the
project's AGPLv3 and commercial licensing model.

## 6. Pull Request Requirements

Every pull request should:

- describe the purpose and scope of the change;
- identify related issues;
- include appropriate tests or validation;
- disclose third-party dependencies or copied material;
- preserve applicable copyright and license notices; and
- confirm completion of the required contributor agreement.

Suggested pull-request confirmation:

> I confirm that I have the right to submit this Contribution and have
> completed the contributor agreement required by `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

This confirmation documents compliance but does not replace the separately
accepted contributor agreement.

## 7. Issues, Discussions, and Suggestions

Opening an issue or participating in a discussion does not assign ownership
of your intellectual property.

By posting an issue, comment, or suggestion, you grant the project owner and
community a non-exclusive permission to read, quote, reproduce, and discuss
that submission for project evaluation and administration, subject to
applicable law and platform terms.

Do not include confidential information or material you do not have
permission to disclose.

## 8. Compensation

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, Contributions are
voluntary.

Contributors are not entitled to:

- royalties;
- equity;
- revenue participation;
- ownership of the project as a whole; or
- compensation arising from commercial, enterprise, governmental, or
  sovereign licensing.

This does not affect any rights expressly granted back to a contributor
under an executed contributor agreement.

## 9. Licensing of Accepted Contributions

Accepted Contributions may be distributed:

- under GNU AGPLv3 as part of the open-source project;
- under separate commercial or enterprise licenses;
- under sovereign or public-benefit licenses; and
- under other terms authorized by the executed contributor agreement.

## 11. No Obligation to Accept Contributions

Submission of a Contribution does not obligate the project owner to review,
accept, merge, maintain, publish, or distribute it.

The project owner may request revisions or decline a Contribution for
technical, licensing, security, provenance, governance, or other reasons.
